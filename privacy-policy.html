TawaKidz Media ("we," "our," or "us") built our mobile applications as commercial, free, or ad-supported apps (each, an "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use any of our Android applications published on the Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or using our Apps, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We design our Apps with user privacy in mind and aim to minimize data collection. Depending on the specific features enabled in the App you are using, the following may apply:

  • Offline & Local Data: All creative artwork, game progress, color palettes, settings, and saved files created within our offline-first Apps are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal creations or gameplay data on external servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, phone number, or email address) unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

Our Apps may utilize standard third-party services provided by Google to distribute the App, ensure stability, or display advertisements (if applicable to the specific App). These third-party providers may collect device identifiers, crash logs, or diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core app distribution, licensing, and system updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob (If Applicable): Some of our Apps may display advertisements. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general interaction data to serve contextual or personalized ads. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics (If Applicable): Used to collect anonymous crash reports, device model info, OS version, and performance diagnostics to help us fix bugs and improve app stability.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on the features of the specific App, we may request limited device permissions solely to support core functionality:

  • Storage / Photos & Media (Optional): Requested only when you choose to save or export completed artwork or images directly to your device's gallery.
  • Network Access (Optional): Used only if the App loads advertisements, checks for updates, or opens external support links. Offline features function without an internet connection.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Apps are intended for a general audience unless explicitly labeled otherwise on the Google Play Store listing. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information via direct contact, please reach out to us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Data Security & Retention

Because gameplay and user-created content remain stored locally on your device, you maintain direct control over your data and can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Any emails sent to our support address are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry.
